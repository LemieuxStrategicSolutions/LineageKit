## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. original record image created near the event
2. official certificate or archival copy
3. reliable derivative with a clear citation
4. index or transcription
5. obituary, newspaper, cemetery database, or published genealogy
6. another person's online tree
7. unsourced family lore

Lower-ranked sources can be valuable leads. They simply require independent confirmation.

## Citation minimum

Every saved source should identify:

- creator or issuing body
- record type or title
- place and date
- collection, volume, page, image, certificate, or file number
- repository or website
- stable URL when available
- access date for online material
- the exact person or claim it supports

## Search discipline

- Search by place, relatives, occupation, and date range as well as name.
- Search spelling variants and phonetic equivalents.
- Inspect the image; do not rely only on an index.
- Record negative searches.
- Separate facts stated by the record from conclusions inferred from it.
