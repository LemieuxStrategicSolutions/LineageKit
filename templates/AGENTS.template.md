## Research rules

- Treat hints, public trees, indexes, and name matches as leads, not proof.
- Record the source for every material fact.
- Distinguish `confirmed`, `probable`, `hypothesis`, `conflicting`, and `disproved` claims.
- Prefer original records or images over transcriptions and other people's trees.
- Never merge two people based on name alone. Use dates, places, relatives, occupations, and records.
- Log negative searches; knowing where a person was not found prevents repeated work.
- Protect living people's addresses, contact details, medical facts, and other sensitive information.

- `Records/` holds record images and transcriptions.
